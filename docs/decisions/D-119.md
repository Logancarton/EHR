# D-119 — Combine medication and prescribing companions under Medications

Decision ID: D-119
Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Accepted
Amends: [D-090](D-090.md), [D-116](D-116.md), [D-117](D-117.md)

## Owner direction

The companion rail must not display two duplicate medication icons/tabs. The higher tab ("Medications" / "Meds") represents the core patient chart medication record and remains pinned at the top of the right companion rail. The lower tab ("Prescribing") represents the cross-patient practice prescribing queue (UI-7d / D-090); it is unpinned from the default companion rail.

## Interaction and authority

1. **Default companion pins:**
   `DEFAULT_PINS.right` includes the 6 core patient record tools (`medications`, `labs`, `documents`, `communication`, `history`, `orders`) followed by practice companions (`calendar`, `ai`, `hr`, `scratchpad`, `tasks`, `calc`). `prescribing` is removed from default pins.

2. **Discoverability preserved:**
   - **`+` Open workspace launcher:** The practice prescribing operations queue remains directly accessible as a major workspace module (`view: "prescribing"` / `PrescriptionOperationsWorkspace`).
   - **"Add a tool" menu:** `prescribing` retains `surfaces: ["full", "panel"]` in the tool registry. Any clinician wishing to keep the practice queue pinned beside their work can pin it to the rail via "Add a tool" (+).
   - **Patient chart prescribing:** Patient-specific prescription staging, drafting, interaction checks, and transmission status remain accessible through the primary `medications` ("Meds") companion and chart section (`PatientPrescriptionWork`).

3. **No loss of clinical capability:**
   Unpinning `prescribing` from the default rail eliminates visual clutter and role confusion while preserving full access to both the cross-patient queue and patient medication records.

## Owner amendment — one Medications companion (2026-10-03)

The owner explicitly requested combining the two right-strip tools and removing Prescribing. This supersedes the optional separate Prescribing pin above. The `prescribing` registry entry retains only its full workspace surface; legacy companion IDs and saved pins normalize to `medications`. The Medications companion offers **Patient medications** (the existing authoritative medication record and patient prescription work) and **Prescribing queue** (the existing `PrescriptionOperationsWorkspace`). Queue counts follow Medications. The queue can still expand/redock beside a chart and open its existing full workspace. No persistence, authorization, transport or clinical truth owner changes.

No-loss inventory: medication review/edit/reconciliation and prescription preparation/authorization/history remain in Patient medications; cross-patient attention counts, integration status, recovery/evidence actions and the full-workspace return path remain in Prescribing queue. The removed rail launcher and pin-menu entry both resolve to the retained Medications entry. Patient order-created events refresh that patient's prescription work after staging.
