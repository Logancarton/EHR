## Decision

The owner requested content correction after reviewing the existing Overview. The default presents current diagnoses and medications first, then last signed plan and next appointment, symptoms/measurements, results and orders, psychiatric history/treatment trials, recent activity, and care coordination. This is Clinical Bond's default, not a claim that every EHR uses one standardized ordering.

Clinical attention remains above and independent of hideable cards. Separate clinical issues from unsigned-note workflow tasks. Critical descriptions stay expanded; other descriptions can be opened, with their actions always visible.

Use existing authoritative sources: clinical snapshot for history, assessments, observations and signed continuity; administrative read for care coordination; permission-aware orders API for recorded order state. Missing history is explicitly unrecorded. History excludes entered-in-error records, retains status/date/source, and links back to the existing History surface. Historical events and dated screening results do not become a current clinical risk formulation. Safety-plan status remains unavailable until its reviewed source contract exists.

Laboratory results display recorded values, dates, status, reference ranges and recorded review evidence. Orders without linked final observations are a literal relationship projection, not proof of delivery, panel fulfillment or clinical completion. Order-read failures do not hide successfully loaded observations; wrong-patient orders/history are rejected and asynchronous responses are cancelled on patient changes.

Reuse the existing card preference/controller lifecycle for the new summaries: reorder, pin, width, collapse, hide and restore. Upgrade the known untouched legacy default order; preserve custom and pinned relative order and insert new summaries before activity. Minimal mode can hide the new summaries with visible recovery. No second router, patient store, task truth or AI subsystem is introduced.

## Boundaries and remaining gaps

This improves presentation and source-grounded access; it is not a deeper clinical reasoning mechanism or an AI expansion. It does not create a safety-plan/risk-assessment record or certify lab-panel completion. Existing uncommitted header/sidebar work is preserved and remains separately owned. No money is spent and only synthetic records are used.
