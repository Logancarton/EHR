## Decision

The owner's plan, decided 2026-09-26, is:

1. **Build a fully formed, functional prototype to pitch for funding.** The closer the prototype is to a working product, the stronger the pitch. Funding pays for AI work, live integrations, production storage/infrastructure, and possibly a team.
2. **Foundation first.** Complete the architecture and the EHR loop on synthetic data. The owner continues building on that foundation himself.
3. **No spending by agents.** Agents must not sign up for, enable, or depend on anything that costs money — paid vendors, subscriptions, hosted services, paid APIs or hosted models — unless Logan explicitly approves it.
4. **Openings for the paid parts.** Every paid integration (DrFirst prescribing, controlled-substance/EPCS, clearinghouse, production storage/hosting, hosted AI models) is designed and built up to its adapter boundary, so that after funding it is connected rather than re-architected. Until connected, it stays clearly disconnected, as the existing honesty rules require.
5. **The pitch covers everything except the live integrations.** Funding comes next, then the integrations.
6. **AI goes in during the final weeks before pitching, by the owner's choice.** Until then, keep the existing AI seams intact (shared planner, permission-aware context, provenance, reviewed proposals) so AI features can be added without restructuring. Do not start AI feature expansion unless Logan directs it.

## Relationship to D-106 and the EHR-first firewall

D-106's long-term product domains and the EHR-first scope firewall still apply. What changes is the milestone the firewall points at. Before, it was "a production-ready psychiatric EHR". Now it is **a functional funding prototype of the EHR loop**:

`Intake -> schedule -> patient chart -> encounter -> note/scribe -> medications/prescribing -> labs/results -> communication -> billing -> follow-up`

The production-readiness gate (real PHI, HIPAA infrastructure, live vendor transport, certification) follows funding. It is not dropped. The prototype must be architected so that gate can be met without a rewrite, and real PHI remains prohibited until it is.

## Consequences

- Prefer work that makes the loop complete, working, and demonstrable on synthetic data over work that polishes the shell or expands deferred domains.
- A slice that would need a paid service stops at the adapter boundary and records what funding must supply.
- Agents follow this plan rather than re-arguing it. A concrete defect or risk in carrying it out is still reported, so the owner can decide.
