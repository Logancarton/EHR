# D-106 — Clinical Bond is the healthcare-practice workspace; the EHR is its clinical core

Decision ID: D-106
Date: 2026-09-26
Architectural status: Active / Accepted
Implementation status: Product direction accepted; documentation alignment only. Workspace implementation remains incremental.

## Decision

Clinical Bond is not limited to an EHR with AI added to it. It is an **AI-native healthcare-practice workspace** whose clinical/EHR system is the authoritative clinical core.

The long-term product domains are:

1. **Intake** — inquiry/new-patient workflow, prospective identity, portal setup, forms/consents, coverage/readiness, scheduling and transition into care.
2. **Provider / Clinical Home** — the provider's primary operating environment for patient context, communication with patients/team/other providers, prescribing, results, notes, telehealth, AI-assisted scribing, clinical analysis, patient homework, teaching/whiteboard tools and reusable teaching material. AI may recommend billing/coding; the authorized provider reviews or changes recommendations before initiating billing.
3. **Billing** — charge/claim workflow, claim status and lifecycle, exceptions/outliers, financial analysis, and AI recommendations. External submission remains unavailable until a real clearinghouse adapter is configured.
4. **Analysis** — a general analysis workspace over authorized clinical, operational, financial and business data. It is a projection/analysis layer, never a competing source of truth.
5. **Branding / Growth** — a centralized workspace for website and supported social/marketing channels plus performance analysis. External publishing or analytics must be backed by real configured integrations and evidence.
6. **HR** — workforce and HR operations with role-appropriate access.
7. **Personal + Shared Storage** — provider/user private storage and organization-shared knowledge for useful PDFs, templates, teaching material and other documents, with explicit ownership and access boundaries.

These are **product domains**, not a requirement to put seven permanent buttons in the shell. The existing browser-like shell, persistent workspace tabs, universal `+` launcher, companion/canvas lifecycle, progressive disclosure and no-permanent-left-rail direction remain valid. A domain may be presented as a major workspace, companion, expandable canvas, or contextual surface according to workflow frequency and context.

D-085/D-086's earlier rule that Staff/HR must not be a major suite workspace is amended: HR is now a first-class product domain. Existing HR companion behavior may remain while the durable suite/workspace presentation is designed and migrated additively. Do not delete or duplicate the working HR system merely to change its placement.

## Provider workspace and patient workspace

`Patient = workspace` remains a core clinical rule, but it is no longer the whole product model. The provider also has a persistent operating workspace that can coordinate work across patients and practice operations. Opening patient-bound work from Provider Home must preserve explicit patient identity and authority.

The provider workspace should converge communication, clinical review and creation rather than recreate each subsystem. Patient charts, medication truth, results, encounters, tasks, messages and billing records retain their existing authoritative owners.

## Ambient AI

AI is a substrate across all product domains. It may retrieve, summarize, compare, draft, scribe, analyze, detect outliers, recommend next actions, help navigate/reconfigure the workspace, and prepare reviewed proposals.

AI does not become the source of clinical, billing, HR, financial or marketing truth. Consequential actions remain explicit, permission-aware and auditable. AI recommendations must identify the authoritative evidence they depend on and must represent missing/uncertain data honestly.

## External ecosystem

Clinical Bond is expected to integrate through replaceable adapters with:

- **DrFirst** for planned prescribing/e-prescribing capabilities;
- an appropriate **controlled-substance / EPCS platform** where required by the eventual prescribing architecture and vendor contract;
- a **healthcare clearinghouse** for claim submission/status/remittance and related billing transport.

No vendor name or planned integration is evidence that it is configured, certified or operational. The application must continue refusing or clearly labeling external actions that lack authoritative transport capability.

## Visual direction

The product should feel like a modern Google/Facebook/Chrome-influenced productivity environment: familiar, light, readable, fast and progressively disclosed. These are interaction and visual references, not a request to clone another product.

The goal is one environment in which a healthcare practice can deliver care, operate the business, understand performance, communicate and grow without fragmenting the user's work across disconnected applications.

## Consequences

- Product language changes from “AI-infused EHR” to “AI-native healthcare-practice workspace with an EHR/clinical core.”
- Future planning must consider both patient workspaces and the provider/practice operating workspace.
- HR, Analysis and Personal/Shared Storage are accepted first-class product domains even if their final UI is not yet implemented.
- Brand expands from a simple website/social destination into a growth/analysis domain.
- Billing evolves beyond internal charges toward a clearinghouse-backed lifecycle, while preserving vendor-neutral transport boundaries.
- Existing clinical safety, provenance, authorization, audit, immutable-record and adapter boundaries remain unchanged.
