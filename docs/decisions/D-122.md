# D-122 — Medication records and frequent actions precede review details

Decision ID: D-122
Date: 2026-10-04
Status: Accepted
Amends: [D-119](D-119.md)

## Decision

The owner accepted medication hierarchy as the next bounded usability slice (MED-HIER-1). In the existing Patient medications view, Current medications places Add medication and New prescription together above active records. New prescription invokes the same patient-bound composer callback previously exposed in Prescription work; it is moved, not duplicated. Prescription lifecycle groups and their explicit authorized actions remain in the existing Prescription work surface.

Active medications precede reconciliation and historical/non-active records. Each active record retains dosing, indication, provenance, monitoring and its existing clinical actions. The medication authority explanation moves to a labeled disclosure below active records. No essential record text is reduced or removed.

Medication reconciliation stays mounted below active records. Its summary reports the retrieved pending/resolved counts. A successful load with pending evidence opens the review; an empty review stays collapsed until the clinician opens it to record a patient report or inspect resolved history. Closing the disclosure preserves its local draft and interpretation. Errors remain visible outside the disclosure, and failed initial retrieval offers Retry. Loading or failed retrieval must not masquerade as zero pending evidence or an empty medication record.

## Ownership and limits

This improves presentation hierarchy, read-state honesty and focused validation. It does not change clinical record ownership, matching rules, reconciliation decisions, prescription authorization, external transport or patient selection. Existing APIs, patient-keyed components, cancellation guards and patient-bound composer actions remain authoritative. No new persistence, registry, router, model, vendor or dependency is introduced.

Disclosure state is local and does not add reload durability for drafts. The existing medication truth revision still remounts record/reconciliation work after a prescription event or truth confirmation. Detached/chart medication surfaces share the retained components; patient-pane identity remains owned by their existing headers. Compact History controls, broader compact tool layouts, full browser acceptance and the synthetic clinic-day gate remain separate work.
