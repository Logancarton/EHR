# D-129 — Forms requested from the chart reuse the portal invitation

Decision ID: D-129
Date: 2026-10-07
Architectural status: Active / Accepted
Implementation scope: Owner request 2026-10-07 (send pre-visit assessments and consents to established patients), part 1 of 3
Amends: [D-109](D-109.md)
Related decisions: [D-107](D-107.md) (no paid transports), [D-127](D-127.md) (patient messages)

## Decision

A clinician can ask an established patient to complete self-report rating scales and sign consents from the chart (**Request forms** beside Compose in Messages, and a link from the compose dialog).

- **One patient-facing model.** A request is an `intake_portal_invitation` with no intake episode, an explicit `requested_items_json` list and the `thread_id` that records it (migration `2026-10-07-002-chart-form-requests`). The same single-subject token and date-of-birth check as the intake packet (D-109) authenticate the patient. NULL items keep the intake packet's meaning.
- **Only what was asked.** The portal shows only the requested scales and consent templates, skips the contact step, and a submission records only requested items: a PHQ-9 link cannot sign an unrelated consent or rewrite the chart's contact details. Completion is per request; earlier chart scores or signatures do not mark today's request complete.
- **Remote scales are PHQ-9, GAD-7 and ASRS v1.1.** C-SSRS is clinician-administered and its positive answers need an immediate response an unattended link cannot give; it is refused.
- **Delivery is honest.** No portal, SMS or email transport is connected (D-107). The request is recorded in a new message thread that says it was not delivered by the system; the link is returned once to the requesting user to pass on themselves. The raw token is never stored or written into the thread.
- **Results return to the thread.** On submission a patient-side message lists each score and signed consent; scores are recorded in the chart's rating-scale history. A positive PHQ-9 item 9 puts a SAFETY line first and raises the thread to urgent; the patient sees 988/911 resources both on the question and on the receipt.

## Rationale and tradeoffs

A second patient-facing form system would duplicate token handling, DOB verification, signature capture and scoring that D-109 already made safe. Generalising the invitation keeps one authority boundary. The thread is the existing place a clinician reviews what a patient sent, so results land where the inbox already looks; a dedicated "outstanding forms" view can follow if needed.

## Not yet done

Structured releases of information (recipient, scope, purpose, expiry, revocation) and a collaborative safety-plan builder whose signed plan can be shared read-only are the next two parts; see ROADMAP. Real delivery belongs behind a communications adapter once funded.
