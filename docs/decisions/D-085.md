## Decision record

Date: 2026-09-20.

Status: accepted.

### Decision

Clinical Bond's durable shell uses four distinct interaction layers:

1. **Home** is the suite launcher and reset point.
2. The persistent browser-style **workspace tab strip** shows what is currently open.
3. The tab-strip **`+` Open workspace** control is the always-available way to open or recover major workspaces without requiring a permanent left app rail.
4. The right **companion/canvas** system hosts contextual tools that can remain minimized, dock beside work, expand into the main canvas, redock, and minimize again without losing state.

The target does **not** introduce a permanent left navigation rail or a replacement full-width work-menu row.

### Major workspaces and suite entities

Home presents three major entities: **Clinical**, **Billing**, and **Brand**.

Clinical owns major workspaces such as Calendar, Patients, Intake, and Documents. Billing is a major workspace. Website and Social Media consolidate under Brand as those capabilities become real.

Staff/People/HR is deliberately **not** a Home entity and is not a major `+` workspace. Staff/HR belongs in the contextual companion/canvas layer, with an expandable presentation for deeper work.

The `+` launcher opens or focuses Home, Calendar, Patients, Intake, Documents, Billing, Brand, and appropriate recent work. Singleton workspaces are focused if already open rather than duplicated. Patient charts continue to open as independent persistent patient workspaces.

### Companion/canvas lifecycle

Contextual tools include, as implemented and authorized, AI, Communication, Tasks, Assessments, compact Calendar, document/form review, Staff/HR, Scratchpad, and Calculators.

Their canonical presentation lifecycle is:

`minimized/icon -> docked right panel -> expanded main canvas -> redocked -> minimized`

Changing presentation must preserve the same feature instance/lifecycle: selected tool or item, patient/recipient target, draft state, filters, scroll, and return path. Expanding a companion must not create a second router, tool store, draft owner, or clinical truth source.

### Migration safety rule

This is an **additive-first migration**, not permission for a wholesale navigation rewrite.

For each old top-navigation destination:

`add replacement -> verify parity/state/focus/recovery -> remove only that old entry`

The first required migration is:

1. Upgrade the existing tab-strip `+` from **Open a patient chart** to **Open workspace**.
2. Make Home and `+` share one destination catalog.
3. Add **Communication** to the right companion/canvas system while the existing **Team** top-bar menu remains.
4. Implement dock/resize/expand/redock/minimize with state preservation.
5. Verify all migrated Team capabilities through the new path with focused browser coverage.
6. Only then remove Team from the top bar.

Practice is then decomposed one child at a time: Billing to its major workspace, Website/Social Media to Brand, Staff/HR to companion/canvas, Settings to profile/preferences, and Reports to an owning workspace when real. Clinical is decomposed only after each child has a verified owner. No group is removed before its children have replacement access.

> **Amended by [D-087](./D-087.md).** "Settings to profile/preferences" described the child by the name the menu gave it. The surface behind it is organization administration — accounts and access — and the practice's default layouts were already under profile/preferences and never moved. Organization administration went to the account menu instead, gated on `manage_organization`.

### Reason

The prior shell made clinicians choose between multiple navigation systems: labeled top work groups, persistent open tabs, Home shortcuts, and right companion tools. The owner wants the tab strip itself to become the durable workspace shelf: major work can stay open or be closed, and `+` is always available to reopen it. Business areas that are not part of moment-to-moment clinical work should not occupy permanent clinical chrome. Contextual tools should appear beside the current work and expand only when needed.

### Constraints

- Preserve Calendar as a first-class workspace over the authoritative appointment store.
- Reuse the existing navigation controller and shell coordination boundary; do not add a parallel router or event bus.
- Every dismissal has an obvious recovery path; Escape/focus behavior remains coherent.
- Preserve readable labels and keyboard access; do not rely on icon memorization.
- The old top navigation remains until its replacement is proven. Visual cleanliness is not evidence of feature parity.
- No permanent left app rail is introduced.
- Staff/HR is a companion/canvas capability, not a major suite app.
- Home and `+` share destination identity/availability so withdrawn or planned tools cannot appear inconsistently.
- External integrations remain honest about disconnected/planned status.

### Consequences

D-084's Level-1 labeled work navigation becomes transitional. The durable shell remains two levels, but Level 1 eventually simplifies to Clinical Bond/Home, omnibox, and account/preferences while Level 2 owns persistent workspace tabs plus the universal `+` launcher. The right companion rail becomes the consistent home for contextual tools and expandable canvases.

Implementation proceeds through the ordered UI slices at the top of `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
