## Decision record

A patient-specific companion may remain visually open while the clinician moves elsewhere,
but it does not inherit whatever chart or practice canvas happens to come to the front.
The tool keeps a visible patient binding and derives a `toolScope` from the foreground
canvas. It can write or stage patient-bound work only when that same patient chart owns
the foreground.

When the bound chart is not active, the tool is parked and labels itself **Inactive Chart
Pinned**. The binding and unfinished responses remain visible so work is not silently
discarded, but chart-bound controls are disabled. A different patient chart cannot
silently inherit an ASRS, PHQ-9, lab order, or other unfinished patient-specific state.

Assessment insertion has an additional destination rule: **Insert** writes only while the
bound patient's Encounter editor is the active chart section. From Documents, Overview,
or another patient section, the first click opens Encounter and tells the clinician to
review the destination; a second explicit click performs the insertion. Practice canvases
cannot insert at all.

Omnibox action pills remain proposals into existing workflow owners. **Stage Refill…**
may open the local staged-order composer, including for controlled substances or
titration medications, but the shortcut itself never invokes a prescribing transport
adapter. External transmission remains behind the order cart's separate review,
attestation, permission, and controlled-substance boundaries.

The browser mental model is also explicit: Ctrl/Cmd+K focuses the omnibox, Alt+W closes
the active in-app tab, and Ctrl+1–9 addresses the logical in-app tab order (9 selects the
last open tab). When patient tabs exceed the readable shelf, system work remains visible
and excess patient charts move into a labeled overflow without being removed from saved
workspace state.

## Evidence

- `app/lib/companion-tool-scope.ts` is the shared scope contract.
- Rating scales and Labs render the same patient-scope banner and gate mutations through it.
- `WorkspaceKeyboardShortcuts` owns global in-app shortcuts rather than scattering handlers.
- Workspace tabs retain logical order metadata even when a patient tab is visually overflowed.
- Focused unit/browser tests cover scope parking, ASRS insertion routing, patient overflow,
  shortcuts, and the staged-only refill route.
