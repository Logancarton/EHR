## Context

Prior to this work, Intake had mature demographic identity, caller-hold, insurance coverage, and document workflow models (D-075 through D-078), but patient-facing intake forms, digital consents, and psychiatric rating scales were incomplete:
1. Standardized rating scales (PHQ-9, GAD-7, ASRS-v1.1, C-SSRS) existed only as post-chart companion tools (D-102), making it impossible to administer pre-chart baseline screening to prospective patients during intake without premature patient chart creation.
2. Consent templates contained minimal metadata without legal agreement terms or attestation disclosures, and consent signature recording lacked support for verifiable digital capture (drawn canvas or typed attestation).
3. Submitted intake forms were opaque stubs without question-and-answer inspection or clinician sign-off notes.
4. Psychiatric safety rules (such as C-SSRS suicidal ideation or PHQ-9 Item 9 suicide endorsement) were not projected into the intake readiness checklist, allowing high-risk clinical presentations to be treated as routine demographic intake.

## Decision

1. **Pre-Chart and Post-Chart Assessment Domain Continuity:**
   Standardized psychiatric rating scale records (`AssessmentRecord`) and underlying persistence (`clinical_assessments`) support both active `patientId` and pre-chart `prospectivePersonId`. Pre-chart assessment stamps safely fall back to `patientId || null` for version/provenance events to prevent foreign key violations on unpromoted callers. When a prospective person is promoted to an active patient chart (`ProspectivePersonService.promote`), `ClinicalRecordRepository.linkAssessmentsToPatient` atomically re-keys prospective assessment records to the newly created patient ID, ensuring full longitudinal continuity from intake screening into the chart.

2. **Standardized Psychiatric Rating Scale Evaluation & Safety Triggers:**
   The intake workspace includes an interactive rating scale questionnaire runner with real-time scoring and clinical severity evaluation for:
   - **PHQ-9**: 9-item depression severity scoring (0–27) with critical alert flag on Item 9 suicide/self-harm endorsement.
   - **GAD-7**: 7-item anxiety severity scoring (0–21).
   - **ASRS-v1.1**: Adult ADHD self-report screening evaluating part-A positive symptom thresholds.
   - **C-SSRS**: Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale assessing suicidal ideation and suicidal behavior severity.
   Any critical safety flag immediately renders an emergency safety alert banner in the intake detail workspace and elevates the intake readiness checklist step (`assessments`) from `"recorded"` to `"review"`.

3. **Verifiable Digital Consent Signatures:**
   Consent templates now include agreement disclosure text (`bodyText`). Consent signatures support three explicit verified capture methods:
   - `drawn_canvas`: HTML5 vector pointer drawing pad capturing smooth pen/touch signatures as base64 data URLs.
   - `typed_attestation`: Typed legal signature accompanied by an explicit, affirmative attestation statement.
   - `staff_attested`: Witnessed staff attestation with actor recording.
   Recorded signatures render a verifiable digital signature receipt with timestamp, signer identity, IP/user-agent, and visual signature proof.

4. **Structured Form Response Inspection & Clinician Sign-Off:**
   Submitted intake forms are rendered in an interactive structured inspector showing all answered questions across personal, medical, and psychiatric sections. Clinicians can review responses and record sign-off notes (`reviewNotes`), transition the submission to `"reviewed"`, and produce audited events (`intake_form_submission_reviewed`).

5. **D-107 Zero-Cost / Synthetic Boundary:**
   In adherence to D-107, all digital signature capture, questionnaire runners, and review workflows run in native HTML5/Canvas and local SQLite persistence with zero paid vendor APIs or hosted SaaS dependencies.

## Consequences

- Intake readiness checklist (`computeIntakeChecklist`) evaluates `assessments`: returning `"needed"` when empty, `"recorded"` when completed without critical safety findings, and `"review"` when safety flags require clinical assessment prior to consultation.
- Prospective patient promotion preserves all completed intake rating scales into the active chart's clinical history without loss of scoring, item responses, or provenance.
- Digital consent signatures produce verifiable receipts in the chart and intake audit log without external electronic signature fees.
