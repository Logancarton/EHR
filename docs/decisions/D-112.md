# D-112 — An intake contact can be messaged before a chart exists

Decision ID: D-112
Date: 2026-09-29
Architectural status: Active / Accepted
Implementation status: Implemented in MSG-INTAKE-1
Amends: [D-077](./D-077.md)
Amended by: —
Supersedes: —
Related decisions: [D-076](./D-076.md), [D-096](./D-096.md), [D-107](./D-107.md), [D-111](./D-111.md)

## Context

Intake is first contact, and much of first contact is messages ("please bring your insurance card"). The Messages companion only worked on a patient chart. On the Intake canvas it said "Return to a patient chart", and the server refused any thread whose owner was not a `patients` row. So the practice could not write to the person the Intake workspace exists for. Provider messages were also stored as `delivered`, and the chart showed "✓ delivered" and "Patient Portal Direct", though no portal or SMS transport is connected.

## Decision

1. **Ownership follows D-077.** A message row names a chart (`patient_id`), an intake contact (`prospective_person_id`), or both, and at least one is required (migration `2026-09-29-001`). Promotion fills in `patient_id` on the same rows, so the conversation is in the chart from the moment the chart exists. It is never copied.
2. **Authority is unchanged.** Messages still use the existing `create_message_thread` and `send_message` actions. The gateway binds a prospect id to organization-scoped prospect access (D-076). A reply must name its thread's own owner. A promoted or archived prospect cannot be written to, and the refusal points to the chart.
3. **No implicit recipient off a chart.** On a canvas that is not a chart, the companion asks who to write to (intake contacts, then patients) and remembers the choice per canvas. A chart open behind the canvas is never assumed (D-096 and the cross-patient rule).
4. **Recorded, not delivered.** Practice-written messages are stored as `queued` and shown as "Recorded · not delivered", with "Delivery: Not connected". Chart-only actions (Save to Chart, clinical summary, sample triage) are not offered for an intake contact.

## Consequences

- The global inbox (`getAllThreads`) joins `patients` and still lists chart threads only. An intake contact's thread is reached from the Intake canvas until promotion.
- Delivery requires a portal or SMS adapter. Until one is connected (D-107), nothing here claims a message reached anyone.
