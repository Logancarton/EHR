## Decision record

Medication surveillance timing is policy, not patient truth and not presentation text.

Clinical Bond resolves each monitoring rule through four layers:

`system starter -> practice default -> provider override -> patient exception`

The system starter establishes a reviewable rule identity and rationale. A practice owner or manager may set the organization's default timing. A prescribing provider may override that timing for their own attention workflow. A provider may create a patient-specific exception only with a documented reason. The most specific saved layer wins and the resulting attention item names its source.

Intervals are split from presentation into persisted settings: monitoring interval, due-soon window, overdue grace period, and enabled/disabled state. Lab and vital-sign rules share the same engine but consume only evidence of their own kind. Missing or failed policy retrieval does not silently fall back to a different interval and assert that a patient is overdue.

Medication monitoring owns medication-surveillance due/overdue state. The legacy synthetic `patient.alert` strings that duplicated that state are presentation-suppressed by exact fixture value; unknown/manual patient alerts remain visible. Safety/critical alerts therefore stay conceptually separate from routine surveillance timing.

Patient exceptions are auditable clinical decisions. Saving or resetting an override records actor, scope, rule, interval values, and patient reason where applicable. The policy engine never orders a test, changes treatment, or transmits anything automatically.

## Evidence

- `app/lib/clinical-protocols.ts` owns rule identity, precedence, and due-state calculation.
- `clinical_monitoring_policy_overrides` persists only local choices, not duplicate clinical observations.
- `/api/clinical-monitoring-policies` binds practice edits to owner/manager authority and provider/patient edits to the authenticated provider.
- Patient Overview derives surveillance attention from the effective policy plus authoritative observations/vitals.
- Settings expose practice defaults, personal overrides, and an active patient's documented exception without duplicating clinical truth.
- Focused unit/browser coverage pins precedence, lab/vital evidence separation, settings reachability, and retirement of the duplicate synthetic banner.
