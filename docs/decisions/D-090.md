## Decision record

Date: 2026-09-21.

Status: accepted.

UI-7's last two children were queued together and are not the same question. D-085 left
both unsettled and asked that each be decided from where the work is actually done. Done
that way they separate cleanly, and only one of them can be finished today.

### Decision 1 — Labs' owner is the `+` launcher

**Labs and Documents are one kind of destination, and one kind of destination gets one
owner.** The same `PracticeQueueWorkspaceShell` renders both; neither is eligible for a
workspace tab; each publishes a standing count of the work it is holding; each row opens
the patient's chart at the matching section. Nothing about Labs argued for a different
surface from the one Documents reached in UI-7a, so it is offered beside it, and the
unacknowledged-result count moved with the destination by UI-7a's rule.

The catalog entry says "practice results awaiting acknowledgement" rather than "Labs".
The chart has a Labs *section* and the practice has a Labs *queue*; treating those two as
the same thing is precisely the mistake [D-088](./D-088.md) records, which left both
practice queues unreachable for three days.

### Decision 2 — Prescribing's owner is the right companion

Prescribing is not a place to go. `PrescriptionOperationsWorkspace` is a **cross-patient
attention queue**: prescriptions whose transport outcome is unknown, interrupted, failed,
conflicted or stuck in callback processing. Every action it offers — retry a transmission,
record investigation evidence, confirm not received, supersede with a verified
transaction — is patient-bound, and the workspace enforces that itself: each action is
gated on the patient's chart being the **active** execution context.

That gate is the whole argument, and it rules out every full-canvas answer:

- A global module and a patient chart cannot both own the active tab. While the
  Prescribing Operations tab is active, no `.browser-tab.active` carries a patient id, so
  `currentActivePatientId()` answers `null` and `patientContextMatches` is false.
- So from inside the workspace, *every* recovery action is disabled and the only enabled
  control is "Activate patient chart" — which calls `openPatient`, which clears the active
  module, which unmounts the workspace the clinician was working from.
- The queue therefore cannot execute its own actions from its own surface. This is true
  regardless of which vendor is connected; it is a property of the shell, not of the data.

The surface that coexists with an open chart is the companion layer. This is the same
structural reason [D-089](./D-089.md) gave for Tasks, reached independently: a capability
whose every action needs a chart alive beside it belongs where a chart can stay alive.

The per-patient half of the prescribing workflow is **already owned and already correct**:
the chart's Medications section renders `PatientPrescriptionWork`, which carries authorize,
send, retry, confirm medication record, renew, accept/decline change, cancel and record
recovery evidence for that patient, and which works because the chart is the active
context by construction. What the practice-level queue adds is the cross-patient question
— "which of my prescriptions never reached a pharmacy?" — which no number of open charts
answers. Both are needed; only their surfaces were wrong.

### Why Prescribing is not implemented here

Because the replacement cannot be exercised, and an unexercised replacement is exactly
what UI-7a and UI-7b were built to catch.

`prescription_transactions` is the queue's only source, and a transmission is refused
*before* a transaction row is written when no prescribing integration is enabled:
`PATCH /api/orders` answers `External prescribing integration is not enabled for adapter
drfirst-placeholder.` No adapter is enabled, and none can be without a commercial
e-prescribing vendor. So the queue is structurally empty in any checkout of this
repository. Its list, empty state and integration notice render honestly; its detail pane,
its patient-context gate, its retry and its evidence forms cannot be reached at all.

A companion built against that would be provable for the shell and unprovable for
everything the menu entry actually reaches. The migration invariant says a destination is
removed after parity is **verified**, not after it is designed, so Prescribing keeps its
menu entry and the Clinical group stays with it.

Standing up a vendor adapter to make the queue non-empty is explicitly **not** the way
round this. A simulated prescribing transport that manufactured transactions would be the
fabricated-transport-evidence failure AGENTS.md forbids, in the one area — controlled
prescribing — where it is least acceptable.

### Consequences

- The Clinical group now holds exactly one child. That is a transitional state, not a
  reason to delete the last route to a workspace: the group is removed when its last
  child has a *proven* owner, which is the rule UI-6 followed to the end on Practice.
- UI-7 cannot close until Prescribing can be exercised. UI-7d is queued with two entry
  conditions rather than one — build the companion, and be able to drive a non-empty
  queue through it — and the second is a dependency on integration work, not on
  presentation work.
- The defect above is real today and is not repaired by this decision. A clinician who
  opens Prescribing Operations, selects an item and presses "Activate patient chart"
  loses the queue. It is recorded as open work in ROADMAP so that it is fixed as part of
  the move rather than papered over inside the module.
