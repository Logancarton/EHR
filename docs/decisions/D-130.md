# D-130 — The chart has three homes and one index

Decision ID: D-130
Date: 2026-10-08
Architectural status: Active / Accepted
Implementation scope: Owner request 2026-10-08 (chart organization, compared against a conventional EHR chart)
Related decisions: [D-116](D-116.md), [D-117](D-117.md), [D-129](D-129.md)

## Context

The owner compared the chart with a conventional EHR whose chart is one left-hand list of sections (facesheet, history, problems, medications, allergies, vitals, notes, demographics, appointments, account, insurance, documents). Ours already held most of that content, but spread across chart sections, the right tool strip and the Patient information drawer, with no single place showing the whole chart. Some content was missing.

## Decision

Keep the workspace model rather than adding app-level left navigation (VIS-03). Give each existing home one job and add a map across them:

- **Chart sections** (Overview, Encounter) hold the clinical record being read and written.
- **The right strip** holds the tools used during a visit (Medications, Labs, Documents, Communication, History, Orders).
- **Patient information** holds the administrative profile: Profile, Additional info, Contact, Related people (including the responsible party/guarantor), Care network, Coverage, Pharmacy, Appointments, Releases, Intake.
- **The Chart index** (the header's former "More chart tools") lists every part of the chart in two groups, Clinical record and Profile, says where each opens, and opens it there. It adds no surface and owns no data; owners are unchanged.

Content filled in the same homes: additional demographics (race and ethnicity in 2024 federal categories, sexual orientation, marital status, previous name, occupation, education, primary provider, referral source; "Declined" distinct from "not asked"); a per-patient appointments list; medical, surgical, family medical, SDOH and implanted-device history beside psychiatric history; ICD-10-CM search (CMS FY2026 valid codes, public domain, shipped in `app/server/reference`); a vitals table; referral and patient-education document types.

## Not done

Account balance and payments wait for P9 (a payment/clearinghouse authority); Rx eligibility waits for a contracted e-prescribing vendor; SNOMED CT needs a licence; patient portal accounts wait for the P7 patient identity model.
