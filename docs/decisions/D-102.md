## Context

An owner review found that opening any right-rail tool slid a panel over the chart. With it open, Review & Sign, the visit-readiness panel and the chart's Message / More / Open encounter actions were all underneath it. Each tool also built its own header, spacing, scroller and bottom button, so the rail read as seven apps. The Calculators panel opened with nine hard-coded PHQ-9 answers keyed so that every item counted as answered, which presented a complete 10/27 score for any patient and let Save to Chart record it. Scratchpad showed another patient's titration memo with no name on it.

## Decision

1. **Push on wide screens, float on narrow ones.** Above 1080px the workspace's right margin grows by the panel width, so the chart shrinks beside the panel as a Google Docs side panel does. At 1080px and below there is no width to give, and the panel keeps the elevated overlay. The expanded (main-canvas) presentation is unchanged.
2. **One frame** (`CompanionPanelFrame`) for every companion: a compact header (tool name, its context once, expand / unpin / close), an optional fixed toolbar, a single scrolling body and a sticky footer for the main action. Tools own only their slot content. Calculators and Scratchpad gain the shared expand/redock control.
3. **Rating scales start blank and are keyed per patient and instrument.** A scale reports "N of M answered" and has no score, Insert or Save until every item is answered.
4. **Scratchpad notes carry their owner.** Each note shows its patient or "Practice note — no patient". With a chart in front of the clinician, only that patient's notes and practice notes show (the rest are counted as hidden); new notes default to that patient and can be switched to practice.
5. **Rail labels are hover/focus tooltips only**, per the owner's direction. The rail stays icons only, and each badge states what it counts ("14 lab results to review") in the tooltip and the accessible description.

## Consequences

- The patient-bound scope banner collapses to one line while the tool's patient is the foreground chart and expands only when parked or unbound, so a panel names its patient once.
- Communication's Team channel footer expands the companion ("Open Full Team Workspace") instead of sending the clinician to the task queue under a Team label. Its Tasks sub-tab links to the practice queue by that name.
- Existing dev databases keep their untagged seeded notes, which now read as practice notes. Fresh seeds tag the clinical memos to Maya Chen.
