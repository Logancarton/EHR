## Context

Following the delivery of standardized clinical rating scales, digital consent signatures, and pre-chart form review in P7 ([D-109](./D-109.md)), the intake workflow required prospective patients to complete onboarding tasks directly before their clinical evaluation. However:
1. Intake tasks (demographic verification, emergency contact collection, consent signing, and standardized rating scales PHQ-9 and GAD-7) were previously operable only within the staff/clinician intake workspace (`/intake`).
2. Exposing patient onboarding required a secure, external-facing patient portal that operates completely isolated from internal EHR chrome (no clinician navigation bar, patient workspace tabs, omnibox, companion drawers, or staff session gates).
3. Under HIPAA and security best practices, public unauthenticated links must not expose patient health information (PHI) or allow unauthorized data submission without identity gating and token protection.
4. Under D-107, this workflow cannot incur SaaS costs, paid SMS/email aggregators, or hosted e-signature services.

## Decision

1. **Tokenized Prospective Self-Service Route & Shell Isolation:**
   A dedicated public onboarding route `/intake/self-service?token=<raw_token>` is established.
   - `isPatientSelfServiceRoute(pathname)` detects self-service routes.
   - Internal EHR shell elements ([`AppChrome`](../app/components/AppChrome.tsx), top navigation tabs, multi-patient window managers, companions) render `null`.
   - The staff authentication gate ([`AuthSessionGate`](../app/components/auth/AuthSessionGate.tsx)) bypasses session checks for self-service routes, preventing redirection to provider login.
   - A dedicated mobile-first, distraction-free stylesheet ([`app/intake-self-service.css`](../app/intake-self-service.css)) governs the portal presentation.

2. **Cryptographic Token Hashing & Expiration Lifecycle:**
   - Invitation tokens are generated using Node `crypto.randomBytes(32).toString("hex")` (256 bits of entropy).
   - Only a cryptographic hash (`SHA-256`) is stored in the database (`intake_portal_invitations.token_hash`), ensuring that database compromises do not leak usable onboarding tokens.
   - Invitations feature configurable expiration lifetimes (default 7 days). Revocation immediately invalidates active tokens.
   - Access attempts against expired, revoked, or non-existent tokens return structured error reasons (`"expired"`, `"revoked"`, `"invalid"`).

3. **Date-of-Birth (DOB) Identity Gating:**
   - To prevent unauthorized access if an invitation link is misdirected or intercepted, self-service invitations can enforce date-of-birth verification (`requires_dob_gate = 1`).
   - The initial public payload provides zero PHI—only the clinic name, token status, and gate requirements.
   - Full demographic facts, consents, and rating instruments are unlocked only after the recipient correctly provides the prospective patient's date of birth.
   - Failed DOB attempts increment a counter (`dob_attempts`). 5 failed attempts permanently lock the token, requiring staff reissuance.

4. **Self-Service Onboarding Wizard & Pre-Chart State Integration:**
   The self-service portal guides prospective patients through a multi-step sequence:
   - **Step 1: Identity Confirmation** — DOB challenge (when required).
   - **Step 2: Contact Information** — Phone, email, address, and emergency contact (name, relationship, phone).
   - **Step 3: Informed Consents** — Electronic review and signature (drawn canvas signature or typed legal attestation) for General Consent for Treatment and Practice Policies.
   - **Step 4: Clinical Screeners** — Self-administered PHQ-9 (depression) and GAD-7 (anxiety). Item 9 endorsement or elevated distress triggers a prominent 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline support alert.
   - **Step 5: Completion Confirmation** — Displays a unique submission confirmation code (e.g. `CB-IN-XXXXXX`) and next steps.
   - Submissions atomically update the prospective person record, record verifiable consent signatures, and persist standardized rating scale assessments.
   - On completion, the invitation status transitions to `"completed"`, locking further submission.
   - The staff intake queue checklist reflects live progress across `contact`, `consents`, and `assessments`.

5. **D-107 Zero-Cost / Synthetic Boundary:**
   All cryptographic token generation, hashing, identity gating, canvas drawing, and scoring run entirely on local runtime primitives (Node `crypto`, SQLite, HTML5 Canvas) without commercial e-signature or onboarding vendors.

## Consequences

- Prospective patients can complete identity, contact, consent, and psychiatric rating scales independently prior to their first appointment.
- Staff maintain full oversight: generating invitation links, viewing expiration/status, and revoking tokens directly from the intake workspace detail pane.
- Clinical safety flags (PHQ-9 Item 9 endorsement) recorded through self-service immediately elevate the intake queue status to `"review"`, surfacing urgent clinical alerts to front-office and clinical staff.
- When the prospective patient is subsequently promoted to an active patient chart, all self-service contact facts, consent signatures, and assessment scores carry forward seamlessly without data loss.
